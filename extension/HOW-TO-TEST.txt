Plainly for Firefox — temporary install

Firefox will not permanently install this unsigned file. Load it as a temporary add-on:

1. Open Firefox.
2. Go to about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox
3. Click "Load Temporary Add-on…"
4. Choose this file (Plainly.xpi), or unzip it and choose manifest.json.
5. Pin Plainly from the extensions menu if you don't see the toolbar button.

The add-on stays installed until you close Firefox.

What to try:
- Click the toolbar button.
- Paste text, or use Policy / News / Science.
- Switch Easy, Plain, and Very easy. The rewrite stays on your device.
- Open a normal article and click "Simplify this page". Close the panel to return to the original.

It cannot rewrite Firefox's own pages (about:, settings, the add-ons site) or many PDFs. Paste those instead.
