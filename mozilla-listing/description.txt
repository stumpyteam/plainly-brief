Plainly turns the article you are reading into a shorter, plainer version.

Open any news story and click the Plainly button. Choose one:

- Plain Summary: a short version of the story
- More Context: a little more of what happened
- Bullet Points: the same idea as a list

The summary opens in a panel over the page. Close it and the original article is still there. A word count shows how long the article was and how long the summary is. The text size is remembered the next time you open it.

Plainly runs on your device. The page is not uploaded, and the add-on does not collect personal data. Your theme and text size stay in Firefox's local extension storage.

It works on ordinary articles. It cannot rewrite Firefox's own pages, such as Settings or the Add-ons site.

Privacy
This add-on does not collect or transmit data. data_collection_permissions is set to "none".
